# Philippine legal, tax & compliance — research brief (2026-09-02)

**Domain:** Philippine registration, tax, labor, privacy, IP and consumer compliance for a small engineering studio (software + hardware), organised as a compliance REGISTER the founders can keep and a script can check.

**Disclaimer.** This is research for a checklist, not legal or tax advice. Every filing, election and signature stays human-gated and should be confirmed with a Philippine CPA / lawyer before it is acted on. Amounts and dates below are only those read from the cited source on 2026-09-02; anything not read from a source is marked **UNVERIFIED**. Items that changed in 2024–2026 are marked **[CHANGED 2024–26]**.

**Provenance grades used:** **A** = official primary text (statute/EO on lawphil.net, agency site/advisory, SC decision, WIPO Lex copy of statute); **B** = Big-4 / major PH firm (PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries, Grant Thornton P&A, Forvis Mazars, ACCRALAW, Baker McKenzie); **C** = P2 knowledge base (Taxumo); **S** = only seen in a search-result snippet of an official page, full text not retrieved (treat as UNVERIFIED).

**Retrieval limits hit:** officialgazette.gov.ph, sec.gov.ph, privacy.gov.ph, bir.gov.ph (JS-only), pagibigfund.gov.ph (reCAPTCHA), ipophl.gov.ph (DNS), chanrobles.com and the Senate/House PDF mirrors all refused automated fetches. Where a statute page on lawphil.net was truncated (RCC Title XIII, LGC Book II, IP Code Part IV on lawphil), the text is either taken from an alternate copy (WIPO Lex for the IP Code) or flagged UNVERIFIED.

## 4. Decision points (trade-offs, sourced)

**Entity type.** *Sole prop* = DTI BN (₱200–₱2,000), personal income tax (graduated or 8%), unlimited liability, no SEC reporting. *OPC/corporation* = SEC incorporation, 20%/25% CIT, separate legal person, SEC GIS/AFS every year (RCC Sec. 25, 177 — 177 UNVERIFIED), no minimum capital (RCC Sec. 12). RCC Sec. 10 allows a single incorporator (OPC). Note the OPC bar on "a natural person who is licensed to exercise a profession… for the purpose of exercising such profession" (Sec. 116 — text UNVERIFIED): an engineering studio that sells *products and software*, not licensed engineering practice, is the safer framing; confirm with counsel. Converting sole prop → corp later means a fresh BIR registration, new permits and new invoices (§6).

**Foreign co-founder.** A domestic-market enterprise with any foreign equity needs US$200k paid-in (US$100k with ≥15 direct Filipino employees / DOST-tech / startup endorsement) unless it is an export enterprise (RA 11647). Selling to consumers = retail → ₱25M paid-up if foreign-owned (RA 11595). List A/B activities (professions, mass media, etc.) UNVERIFIED because EO 175's annex was not retrievable.

**VAT vs non-VAT.** Below ₱3M gross you may stay non-VAT and pay 3% percentage tax (2551Q) or elect 8%. Above ₱3M in any 12 months VAT registration is mandatory (Sec. 236(G)); voluntary registration locks you in for 3 years (Sec. 236(H)). Corporate clients that are top withholding agents withhold 1%/2% regardless; VAT-registered buyers can only claim input VAT on a proper **invoice** (not an unstamped OR) — so B2B credibility often favours VAT registration even below threshold (trade-off, human decision).

**8% vs graduated.** 8% is on gross sales above ₱250k with no deductions, in lieu of income tax *and* percentage tax; irrevocable for the year; not available if VAT-registered. Graduated + itemised/OSD favours low-margin hardware resale; 8% favours high-margin services. Clients still withhold 5%/10% (2307) and you credit it on 1701Q/1701.

**BMBE.** Income-tax exemption on operating income and minimum-wage exemption (RA 9178 Sec. 7–8), asset ceiling ₱3M excl. land, CA every 2 years; not an exemption from VAT/percentage tax or from SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG. Cheap (≤ ₱1,000) and reversible; verify with the RDO how the exemption is annotated on the COR (procedure UNVERIFIED).

**Employees vs contractors.** Four-fold test with control as the decisive element (Sonza). Contractors: you withhold EWT (5%/10% or 2%), no SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG/13th-month; but if you control means and methods the relationship is employment "by law, not by contract label", with back benefits. Employees: register with SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG within 30 days (SSS), OSH programme, 13th month by 24 Dec.

**Selling online.** ITA adds registration, disclosure and complaint-handling duties and ₱20k–₱1M fines; marketplaces will demand your BIR COR and IDs for KYC (IRR).

## 5. Mechanical gate candidates

**Register schema (one row per obligation; YAML/CSV in repo):**

```
id, obligation, category, applies_if (boolean expr over profile: entity_type, has_employees, gross_sales_12m, vat_registered, sells_online, foreign_equity, total_assets),
agency, cadence (once|monthly|quarterly|annual|event), deadline_rule (e.g. "quarter_end+25d", "Jan-20", "Dec-24", "hire_date+30d"),
evidence_type (filed_return|receipt|certificate|contract|register), evidence_path, evidence_date, evidence_hash,
status (na|pending|done|overdue), owner, requires_professional_signoff (bool), signoff_path,
source_url, source_grade (A|B|C|S), verified_on, notes
```

**What a script can check (fail-closed):**
1. Schema validity; every row has `source_url` and `source_grade`; rows graded S/UNVERIFIED cannot be marked `done` without `notes` explaining the human verification.
2. `applies_if` evaluated against the company profile file → set `status=na` or `pending`; profile changes (first hire, gross_sales crossing ₱3M in trailing 12 months, first online sale, foreign investor) automatically activate rows.
3. For every applicable row: `evidence_path` exists, is non-empty, hash recorded, `evidence_date` ≤ computed deadline; else `overdue`.
4. Deadline engine: compute next due date from `deadline_rule` + fiscal calendar; emit warnings at T-30/T-7/T-0; block the phase gate when any applicable row is `overdue`.
5. Freshness: `verified_on` older than 12 months → row flagged "re-verify source" (laws in this domain changed in 2018, 2021, 2024, 2025).
6. Consistency checks: VAT-registered → 2550Q rows active and 2551Q inactive, and vice-versa; 8% election → 2551Q inactive; has_employees → SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG/13th-month/OSH rows active; entity_type=corp → SEC rows active; gross_sales_12m > 3M → audited-FS row active; sells_online → ITA rows active.
7. Invoice control: sequential invoice numbers within ATP range; every sale ≥ ₱500 has an invoice record.
8. Withholding control: for each client payment, expected 2307 exists and matches rate (5/10/2/1%) and quarter; sworn declarations sent before 15 Jan.

**Must remain human / professional sign-off (script may only verify that the signed artefact exists):** choice of entity and capital structure; VAT / 8% elections; BMBE application; every tax return and payment (signed by taxpayer/officer); AFS audit opinion (independent CPA); SEC GIS/AFS submissions; employment vs contractor classification; contracts, NDAs, IP assignments and any e-signature; NPC registration/DPO appointment; LGU renewal; any interpretation of a rule marked UNVERIFIED.

## 6. Early irreversibles (costly to undo)

1. **Entity type** — sole prop → corporation requires new SEC registration, new BIR COR/TIN for the corporation, new permits, new invoices/ATP, and transfer of contracts; DTI BN and old registrations must be closed (closure procedures UNVERIFIED).
2. **Voluntary VAT registration** — 3-year lock-in (Sec. 236(H)) [A].
3. **8% election** — irrevocable for the taxable year (Sec. 24(A)(2)(b)) [A].
4. **TIN** — one per person for life; a corporation's TIN is separate (multiple-TIN penalty UNVERIFIED).
5. **Corporate/business name and trademark class** — DTI BN scope (barangay/city/regional/national) fixes where the name is protected; SEC name rules (MC 13-2019) apply; trademark DAU clock starts at filing (3 years).
6. **Foreign equity structure** — capital thresholds (US$200k/100k; ₱25M retail) must be met at formation; restructuring later is a capital amendment.
7. **Books and invoicing method** — manual vs loose-leaf vs CAS: CAS/POS needs BIR acknowledgment/reconfiguration (RR 7-2024 deadlines already passed); printed invoice stock under ATP.
8. **Fiscal year** — individuals are calendar-year (PwC); corporations choosing a fiscal year change it only with BIR approval (UNVERIFIED).
9. **Employment contracts** — once an engagement behaves like employment (control), statutory benefits attach retroactively (Sonza test).
10. **IP clauses** — without a written stipulation the *creator keeps copyright* of commissioned work (Sec. 178.4); retrofitting assignments from ex-contractors is hard.
11. **OPC nominee/alternate nominee** and self-appointed treasurer bond (RCC Sec. 122/124 — UNVERIFIED text) are set at incorporation.
